# 📊 Telco Customer Churn Dashboard

## 🔎 Project Overview

This Excel project analyzes customer churn for a telecommunications
company. The dashboard brings together customer KPIs, churn-rate
analysis, MRR analysis, and interactive slicers to make the main
retention patterns easy to understand.

## 📌 Dashboard KPIs

  KPI                                       Result
  ----------------------------- ------------------
  Total Customers                        **7,032**
  Churned Customers                      **1,869**
  Churn Rate                            **26.58%**
  Active Customer MRR             **\$316,530.15**
  Avg. Tenure --- Churned         **17.98 months**
  Avg. Tenure --- Non-Churned     **37.65 months**

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 📋 Churn Rate by Contract

  Contract           Churn Rate
  ---------------- ------------
  Month-to-month     **42.71%**
  One year           **11.28%**
  Two year            **2.85%**

💡 **Insight:** Month-to-month customers have the highest churn rate,
while two-year contract customers have the lowest.

### 🌐 Churn Rate by Internet Service

  Internet Service        Churn Rate
  --------------------- ------------
  Fiber optic             **41.89%**
  DSL                     **19.00%**
  No internet service      **7.43%**

💡 **Insight:** Fiber-optic customers show the highest churn rate among
the internet-service groups.

### 💳 Churn Rate by Payment Method

  Payment Method                Churn Rate
  --------------------------- ------------
  Electronic check              **45.29%**
  Mailed check                  **19.20%**
  Bank transfer (automatic)     **16.73%**
  Credit card (automatic)       **15.25%**

💡 **Insight:** Electronic-check customers have the highest churn rate
by payment method.

### 👥 Customer Churn Distribution

-   **Non-churned:** 73.42%
-   **Churned:** 26.58%

💡 **Insight:** More than one quarter of the analyzed customer base has
churned.

### 💰 Churned Customer MRR

-   **Total MRR:** \$455,661.00
-   **Active Customer MRR:** \$316,530.15
-   **Churned Customer MRR:** **\$139,130.85**
-   **Churned MRR share:** **30.53%**

💡 **Insight:** Customers who churned were associated with **\$139.13K
in monthly recurring charges**, representing **30.53% of the total
monthly charge base**.

### ⏱️ Tenure Analysis

-   **Churned customers:** 17.98 months average tenure
-   **Non-churned customers:** 37.65 months average tenure

💡 **Insight:** Churned customers have substantially shorter average
tenure than customers who remain.

## ⭐ Key Findings

1.  📉 Overall churn is **26.58%**.
2.  📋 **Month-to-month contracts** have the highest churn rate at
    **42.71%**.
3.  💳 **Electronic check** has the highest payment-method churn rate at
    **45.29%**.
4.  🌐 **Fiber optic** has the highest internet-service churn rate at
    **41.89%**.
5.  ⏱️ Churned customers have much shorter average tenure (**17.98
    vs. 37.65 months**).
6.  💰 Churned customers are associated with **\$139.13K in monthly
    recurring charges**.

## 💼 Business Takeaway

The dashboard suggests that retention efforts should focus particularly
on **month-to-month customers, electronic-check customers, fiber-optic
customers, and shorter-tenure customers**, as these groups show higher
observed churn rates.

> ℹ️ These findings describe relationships observed in the data and do
> not by themselves prove causation.
